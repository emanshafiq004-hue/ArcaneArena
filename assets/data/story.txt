You were not born..You were summoned.
Somewhere between the last breath and the first silence,
the Veillands called your name.

They call you the Veilwalker.
A rare soul who can see what others cannot:
the spirits, the shadows, the forgotten things that drift
between what was and what remains.

The Veil Gate has cracked.
The Shadow Sovereign stirs.
Corrupted spirits flood the forest, the temples, the mountains.

You alone can walk the boundary.
You alone can restore the balance.

The Veillands await, Veilwalker.
What echoes will you leave behind?
